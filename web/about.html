03 / BEHIND THE MISSION

Why find Earth's twin for Mars?

A Mars drill cannot be tested everywhere on Mars. Planetary Twin helps researchers identify places on Earth with comparable ground conditions, so they can investigate promising locations for field testing.

01 / SELECT
◎

Pick a spot on Mars

Choose coordinates on the globe and select one of four depth bands, from surface skin (0–10 cm) to experimental deep drilling (>3 m). The location snaps to a 2° grid.

02 / COMPARE
≋

Compare ground conditions

The matching pipeline compares factors such as temperature, ice, ground class, hardness, rock type and terrain. Its default method is k-nearest neighbours (kNN).

Explore Earth analogs

See ranked Earth regions, match scores, key similarities and differences, and any available field-campaign references. Results depend on backend data availability.
